## Important CRS Changes Effective November 3, 2026

IRCC introduced a new Labour Market Integration factor on November 3, 2026. It changes how points are awarded for Canadian work experience and qualifying high-wage job offers.

Candidates can receive points for either Canadian work experience or a qualifying high-wage job offer, whichever provides the higher score. They cannot receive both.

### Additional points for high-wage Canadian work experience

Candidates with at least 12 months of qualifying Canadian work experience in eligible high-wage occupations may receive:

* 70 points for occupations at least twice the national median wage.
* 40 points for occupations at least 1.5 times the national median wage.
* 20 points for occupations at least 1.3 times the national median wage.

### What happened to LMIA requirements?

As of November 3, 2026, an LMIA is no longer required for a job offer to qualify for these Express Entry points.

However, job offers may still be relevant to eligibility under certain immigration programs.

Candidates should review their Express Entry profiles and follow IRCC's current instructions.
