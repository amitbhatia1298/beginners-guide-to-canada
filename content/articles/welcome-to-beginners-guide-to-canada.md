\# Welcome to Beginners Guide to Canada!



Moving to Canada is an exciting journey, but getting settled can sometimes feel overwhelming. That's why we created Beginners Guide to Canada — to make information easier to find and understand.



\## What You'll Find Here



\*\*Immigration & Visas\*\*



Understand Canadian immigration programs, work permits, permanent residence, and citizenship.



\*\*Jobs & Careers\*\*



Explore practical tips for job searching, creating a Canadian-style resume, and preparing for interviews.



\*\*Banking & Finance\*\*



Learn about Canadian banking, credit scores, budgeting, taxes, and saving money.



\*\*Housing\*\*



Find information about renting, buying a home, and understanding housing costs.



\*\*Life in Canada\*\*



Discover helpful information about healthcare, education, transportation, and everyday Canadian life.



\## Our Mission



Our goal is simple: to help newcomers make informed decisions and build a successful life in Canada.



Whether you're planning your move or already living here, Beginners Guide to Canada is here to help you navigate your journey.
