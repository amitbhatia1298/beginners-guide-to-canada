The story behind BG2C

Built from a real newcomer journey.

Clear guidance. Real experience. Official sources.

Beginners Guide to Canada, or BG2C for short, is a free resource for people who are planning to move to Canada, are going through the process, or have just landed.

I came from India, studied operations management in New Zealand and worked in inventory control there for a few years. In 2019 I moved to Canada as a permanent resident, and in 2023 I became a citizen. Each stage had its own forms, rules and waiting, and finding information I could rely on was part of every one of them. BG2C is the guide I wanted at each of those points.

I am not a lawyer or a licensed immigration consultant. I share what I learned, point to the official source and say when something is only my experience.
