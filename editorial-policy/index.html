Editorial Policy

Beginners Guide to Canada (BG2C) is an independent information resource for people planning to move to Canada and those building a life here. This policy explains how we choose topics, research and prepare articles, handle corrections and disclose the use of technology and commercial relationships.

1. Our purpose and editorial approach

Our aim is to make information about Canadian immigration and everyday life clear, practical and easier for newcomers to understand. We publish guides, explainers, news updates, comparisons and articles about topics such as work, study, housing, banking, taxes, healthcare and settlement.

We aim for accuracy, clarity, usefulness and transparency. We distinguish factual reporting from explanation, personal experience and opinion. We do not publish an article simply to meet a quota or to create pages for search engines.

2. Research and source selection

We research articles using sources relevant to the subject. For government rules and programs, we prioritize primary and official sources, including:

  • Government of Canada and Canada.ca.
  • Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC).
  • Canada Revenue Agency (CRA).
  • Employment and Social Development Canada (ESDC).
  • Provincial, territorial and municipal government websites.
  • Statistics Canada and other authoritative public institutions.

We may also consult reputable publications, professional organizations and other knowledgeable sources for context. Secondary reporting does not replace official confirmation when an article describes a government rule or requirement. We aim to link readers to the original source so they can check details themselves.

3. Fact-checking and changing information

Before publication, we check material claims against reliable sources, with particular care for eligibility rules, dates, fees, deadlines, processing information, statistics and policy changes. We distinguish proposals and announcements from rules that are already in effect. Where requirements vary by province, territory, program or personal circumstances, we explain that difference when relevant.

Information can change after publication. For articles about changing rules or programs, we aim to show a publication or update date and, where appropriate, a “last verified” date. A date does not mean that every detail will remain current indefinitely. Readers should confirm important requirements with the responsible authority before acting.

4. Writing, originality and use of AI tools

BG2C articles are prepared for our readers and should provide original explanation, context or practical value. We do not copy or lightly rewrite another publisher's article. When we refer to another source, we attribute it and link to it where appropriate.

AI-enabled tools may be used to assist with research organization, outlining, drafting, editing or other production tasks. They do not replace editorial responsibility. Material facts, figures, quotations, links and important claims must be checked against reliable sources before publication. We do not knowingly publish fabricated sources, invented quotations, false personal experiences or credentials, or unverified claims generated by a tool.

We review content for accuracy, clarity, originality and usefulness. We do not claim that an article has been independently peer-reviewed, professionally certified or reviewed by a lawyer, immigration consultant, accountant or healthcare professional unless that review has actually taken place and is clearly identified.

5. High-impact topics

Immigration, legal, financial, tax and healthcare information can have significant consequences. We take particular care with these subjects, link to authoritative sources and explain important limits or exceptions where we can verify them. BG2C is an informational publication, not a provider of individualized professional advice. Readers should consult the appropriate qualified professional or government authority about their own circumstances.

6. Updates and article reviews

We may update an article when a rule changes, an official source is revised, an error is identified, or additional information would materially improve the page. Updates may include correcting facts, replacing outdated links, clarifying explanations or adding context. We do not promise a fixed review schedule for every page. Readers should check the relevant official source for the latest requirements, especially before submitting an application or making a financial, legal or healthcare decision.

7. Corrections and reader feedback

We welcome reports of factual errors, outdated information, broken links and unclear explanations. If you identify a problem, email beginnersguidetocanada@gmail.com and include the article URL, the passage in question and a reliable source or explanation if available.

We review correction requests and, where a change is warranted, update the article. Significant corrections or changes may be noted in the article. We do not guarantee a particular response time, but we aim to address credible reports responsibly.

8. Authors, experience and expertise

BG2C may draw on the founder's personal newcomer experience where it is relevant, and we identify personal experience as such. Personal experience is not a substitute for official requirements or professional advice. We do not invent author qualifications, professional designations, interviews, expert reviews or first-hand testing. Where a qualified external reviewer contributes to an article, that person's role and relevant credentials should be accurately described.

9. Advertising, affiliate links and sponsored content

BG2C may display advertising, use affiliate links or publish sponsored material. We aim to identify sponsored content and disclose material commercial relationships clearly. Advertising and commercial arrangements should not determine factual conclusions or override our commitment to accurate, useful information. Any product or service mention should be assessed by readers against their own needs and the provider's terms.

10. Independence and government affiliation

BG2C is an independent website and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by the Government of Canada, IRCC or a provincial or territorial government. Links to official resources are provided to help readers verify information directly.

11. Contact the editorial team

For corrections, feedback or questions about this policy, email beginnersguidetocanada@gmail.com.
