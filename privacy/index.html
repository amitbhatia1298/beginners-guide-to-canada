Privacy Policy

Beginners Guide to Canada (“BG2C”, “we”, “our” or “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information may be collected when you visit beginnersguidetocanada.ca, how it may be used, and the choices available to you.

Information you provide

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address, name and message. We use it to respond and handle your request. Please do not send sensitive personal, financial or immigration documents unless we specifically explain a secure way to do so.

Information collected when you visit

When you visit the Website, the hosting and security services used to deliver it may automatically process technical information such as your IP address, browser and device details, requested pages, referring page, and request date and time. This information may be recorded in server, diagnostic or security logs and used to deliver the Website, prevent abuse, troubleshoot issues and maintain security. The exact log data and retention periods are controlled in part by the hosting provider.

Analytics

At the time this policy was reviewed, the website source code in our repository did not contain an installed Google Analytics or other third-party analytics tracking script. We therefore do not describe analytics tracking as currently active. If analytics is added later, this policy will be updated to identify the service and explain relevant choices before or when it is enabled, as required by applicable law. You can read how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. The current website source code review did not identify an installed advertising or analytics cookie script. Hosting, security, embedded content or other technical services may nevertheless use strictly necessary cookies or similar technologies to provide or protect a service. You can review and manage cookies through your browser settings. If we add non-essential analytics or advertising technologies, we will review applicable consent requirements and update this policy and the site's consent controls before enabling them where required.

Advertising

The Website does not currently contain Google AdSense ad code in the website source code reviewed for this policy update. We may apply to use advertising services in the future. If advertising is introduced, advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure or personalize ads, subject to applicable settings and consent requirements. We will update this policy and implement any required disclosures and consent mechanisms before or when advertising is enabled. For information about Google's advertising practices and controls, see Google's advertising technologies.

How we use information

  • To operate, maintain and secure the Website.
  • To respond to messages and questions you send us.
  • To diagnose technical issues and improve the Website.
  • To comply with legal obligations and address misuse or security issues.

When information may be shared

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that host, deliver or secure the Website, or by email providers when you contact us. We may disclose information where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect our rights, visitors or the Website. We do not currently describe analytics or advertising providers as active unless those services have actually been added. Third-party providers handle information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Retention and security

We keep messages and technical records only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, security, and legal requirements. No website or email transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Please avoid sending sensitive information by ordinary email.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. If we introduce analytics, advertising or other optional tracking, we will provide any additional choices or consent controls required by applicable law. If you have a question about this policy or want to make a privacy request, email beginnersguidetocanada@gmail.com. Please describe your request and the information it concerns. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

Children

This website is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. Please contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information to us.

Third-party websites

Our pages may link to government websites and other third-party sites. We do not control their content or privacy practices. Review their policies before providing information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site and its services change. The “Last updated” date at the top indicates when the policy was most recently revised.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact beginnersguidetocanada@gmail.com.
